How is it made? Where is it from?

Cold-pressed in a slow (masticating) juicer. No heat, no pasteurisation.

  • Fast centrifugal juicers heat and oxidise the juice. Slow pressing keeps more of the natural plant compounds.
  • Ginger and turmeric come from a small biodynamic farming collective in Peru. Lemons from Sicily.
  • Made in Copenhagen in small batches.
  • Third-party tested by the independent lab ALS Global.
Say it in Danish

“Det er koldpresset og hverken varmebehandlet eller pasteuriseret. Ingefæren og gurkemejen kommer fra et biodynamisk landbrugskollektiv i Peru.”

How do I take it?

2 cl a day, neat or mixed with water, tea or juice.

  • Start by mixing it with water. It’s strong. Take it neat once the stomach is used to it.
  • Also nice in a smoothie.
  • Optional tip from the brand: take breaks, e.g. 6 weeks on and 2 weeks off, or 3 weeks on and 1 week off.
Say it in Danish

“Tag 2 cl om dagen, rent eller blandet med vand, te eller juice. Start gerne med at fortynde den, for den er kraftig.”

What does it do for me?

Sell it as a strong, clean daily ritual. Don’t promise health effects.

  • Ginger naturally contains gingerol. Turmeric naturally contains curcumin. Both are plant compounds with antioxidant properties.
  • It’s a pure alternative to sugary shop shots. No apple or orange juice to bulk it out.
  • Not heat-treated, so the natural plant compounds and enzymes are kept.
CarefulThere are no EU-approved health claims for ginger or turmeric. Don’t say it “boosts the immune system”, “fights inflammation” or “prevents colds”.

How is it made?

Pressure-cooked for 4–5 hours, in a process developed with the Danish Technological Institute.

  • Short pressure cooking protects collagen and hyaluronic acid, which suffer from very long heating.
  • It also gives less chance for histamine to build up than 16+ hours of home simmering. Their histamine tests support this.
  • The project was funded by Innovation Fund Denmark’s Innobooster programme.
  • Third-party tested by ALS Global for heavy metals and allergens.

“Den er trykkogt i 4–5 timer i en proces, vi har udviklet sammen med Teknologisk Institut. Det bevarer kollagenet og hyaluronsyren bedre end lang kogning.”

Organic or biodynamic?

Both are organic. Biodynamic goes one step further.

  • Organic: no pesticides or artificial fertiliser, focus on animal welfare. The bones come via Spis Min Gris (Mikkel Hesselager).
  • Biodynamic (Demeter): regenerative farming where the farm is one ecosystem. The bones come from Hedeagergaard.
  • Same nutrient content. Biodynamic costs a little more: 2 glasses are 138 kr vs 158 kr.

Read before Sunday

In the EU you may only use approved health claims for food and supplements. Describe the product; don’t promise cures.

Why choose it

  • Talking about ingredients, origin, process and taste is always safe.
  • Use “contributes to” (bidrager til) for the approved claims.
  • Never say a product cures, treats or prevents anything.

The basic rule

Only EU-approved health claims are allowed. Everything else: describe, don’t promise.
